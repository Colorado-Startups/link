# Governance

The maintainer listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md) owns the project.

| Decision | Who decides |
|---|---|
| An idea becomes an issue | A maintainer, in Discussions |
| A PR merges | A maintainer, after the required checks (CI and DCO) pass |
| Product, vision, the license, these rules | The maintainer, by pull request |

## The maintainer seat

| Question | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who may ask | A contributor with five merged pull requests over three months who keeps the code of conduct |
| Who decides | The maintainer |
| What the seat can do | Review and merge PRs; turn ideas into issues; welcome new contributors |
| How it ends | Stepping down at any time, or six months with no review; the maintainer can end it |

Every seat is held by a person.
